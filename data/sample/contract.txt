SERVICE AGREEMENT

This Service Agreement is between ABC Technologies and XYZ Solutions.

1. Payment Terms
The client must pay all invoices within 30 days of receiving the invoice.

2. Termination
Either party may terminate this agreement by providing 30 days written notice.
Immediate termination is permitted if either party commits a material breach
and fails to correct the breach within 15 days of receiving written notice.

3. Confidentiality
Both parties must keep confidential information private during the agreement
and for two years after the agreement ends.
